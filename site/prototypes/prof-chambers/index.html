Profile

Christian Bird is a Senior Principal Researcher at Microsoft Research, where he has spent sixteen years studying how software is built at scale. With more than twenty-five years of combined industrial software engineering and research experience, his work centers on the empirical analysis of source code, version control history, and software development practice.

He has authored more than 120 peer-reviewed publications on software authorship and ownership attribution, code cloning and reuse, version control forensics, code review, defect and reliability measurement, and AI-assisted code generation. Eight of his papers have received test-of-time, most-influential, or impact awards from ACM and IEEE. He is a named inventor on thirteen granted U.S. patents in source code analysis and developer tooling.

He received the ACM SIGSOFT Early Career Research Award in 2017, was named an ACM Distinguished Scientist in 2024, and is a Senior Member of the IEEE. Before his research career he spent five years as a software engineer at Motorola building embedded development tools. He holds a Ph.D. and M.S. in Computer Science from the University of California, Davis, and a B.S. in Computer Science from Brigham Young University.
