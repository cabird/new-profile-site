Contents

    This site is the record of twenty years spent studying how software is actually built. I am a Senior Principal Researcher at Microsoft Research, where my work centers on the empirical analysis of source code, version control history, and engineering practice at scale — mining repositories, reviews, builds, and telemetry; surveying and interviewing the people who produce them; and building tools that product teams really adopt.

    What follows is the complete corpus, arranged by subject and annotated. Every paper carries a brief plain-language note on the problem it takes up, how it was studied, and what was found. Each is available in full, and each can be interrogated directly — an experiment in what a body of research should let you do.
