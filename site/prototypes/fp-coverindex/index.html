For twenty years I have studied how software is actually built. My work centers on the empirical analysis of source code, version control history, and engineering practice at scale — mining repositories, reviews, builds, and telemetry; surveying and interviewing the engineers who produce them; and building tools that product teams genuinely adopt.

I've worked on code review, branching and release strategy, build systems, developer productivity, and most recently AI-assisted software engineering — always starting from how developers really work, and always with the aim of making that work better.
