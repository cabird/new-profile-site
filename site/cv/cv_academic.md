## Research Interests

Dr. Christian Bird is a Senior Principal Researcher at Microsoft Research, where he studies software engineering practices, developer productivity, and the intersection of AI and software development. His work combines empirical software engineering, large-scale data analysis, and human factors to understand how developers work and how tools can better support them.
