> Yes, user experience actually does matter—even when the users happen to be developers. Accordingly, a substantial user study was launched in this instance, where the subjects of the study were members of MSR’s own technical staff. Another interesting aspect was that the study participants were presented with code samples extracted from their own work. The significance of this, of course, was that it involved the use of not only real-world examples but also ones where all the tradeoffs and implications associated with each important decision point were sure to be fully appreciated by the study subjects. Which is to say that the exercise proved to be an interesting learning experience for all parties involved.
